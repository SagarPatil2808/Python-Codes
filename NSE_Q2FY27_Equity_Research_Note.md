# NSE Ltd — Q2 FY27 Equity Research Note

**Rating: HOLD** | CMP ₹1,804 (BSE, 03-Nov-2026) | Fair value (base) ~₹1,700 | Mkt cap ₹4.46 lakh cr | Results declared 03-Nov-2026 — first as a listed company

---

## 1. The headline is misleading. Ignore the +23% profit growth.

Reported PAT rose 23% YoY to ₹2,578 cr. That is almost entirely a **base effect**: the year-ago quarter carried a ₹1,297 cr SEBI settlement charge; this quarter carried nil. Strip the one-offs out and underlying growth is ~9–11%, not 23%.

| ₹ cr (consolidated) | Q2 FY27 | Q1 FY27 | Q2 FY26 | QoQ | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue from operations | 4,067 | 4,560 | 3,677 | **-11%** | +11% |
| Total income | 4,554 | 5,252 | 4,160 | -13% | +9% |
| Total expenses | 1,161 | 1,129 | 2,354 | +3% | -51% |
| Reported PAT | 2,578 | 3,120 | 2,098 | **-17%** | +23% |
| **Normalised PBT** | **3,425** | 4,101 | 3,134 | -16% | **+9%** |
| Normalised EBITDA margin | 76% | 78% | 76% | — | flat |

EPS ₹10.41 (not annualised); H1 FY27 EPS ₹23.02 vs ₹20.29.

**The real story is sequential.** Revenue fell 11% QoQ while costs stayed flat, so operating leverage ran in reverse and the margin gave back ~2pp (78% → 76%). Every equity product shrank: cash ADV -13%, futures -19%, options premium -25%.

## 2. Why the quarter was weak: a regulatory accident, now being fixed

SEBI's **Closing Auction Session (CAS)**, live from 03-Aug-2026, had a design flaw — index options settled on a closing print that option writers could not hedge into, because the underlying stocks stopped trading 15 minutes earlier. Expiry-day option writing became unpriceable and volumes collapsed industry-wide (-17% to -25% premium turnover).

Critically, **this was a market-size shock, not a share loss**: NSE held 68% of options premium, BSE went 31.5% → 32.6%.

SEBI's final framework (circular 15-Oct-2026, effective **07-Dec-2026**) restores settlement to the last-30-minute continuous-trading VWAP. The hedging gap is closed. October 2026 already saw industry premium turnover rebound ~43% MoM, so Q3 should be sequentially much better.

## 3. Business mix: one product still pays the bills

|---|---|---|---|
| Options (of which weekly Nifty ₹1,699 cr) | 2,216 | 54.5% | +6% |
| Cash market | 474 | 11.7% | +30% |
| Futures & NSEIX | 328 | 8.1% | -3% |
| Data connectivity | 267 | 6.6% | **-9%** |
| Operating investment income | 237 | 5.8% | +9% |
| Data feed & terminals | 165 | 4.1% | +43% |
| Listing services | 150 | 3.7% | +70% |

**Winners:** listing fees (+70%, IPO boom — 70 mainboard IPOs in H1 vs 54), data feed (+43%), clearing & settlement (+33%), mutual-fund platform (orders +155% YoY, share 18% → 22%), commodities (H1 ADT ₹337 cr, ~4.3x YoY; crude oil options +448% YoY), currency derivatives (+150%).

**Problems:** monthly non-Nifty index options -21% YoY (SEBI expiry rationalisation); futures -3% despite ~100% share; and **data connectivity -9% YoY for a second straight quarter** even as colocation racks rose to ~1,868 — management now confirms a rack *surplus*. Surplus capacity with falling revenue is not a good combination, and they would not explain it.

The fast-growing lines compound 30%+ but are together only ~11% of revenue. They cannot rescue a soft options quarter yet. If they reach 15–20%, earnings become materially less regulation-sensitive — that mix shift is the thing to track.

## 4. Costs: the first-ever guidance, and the one line that matters

Technology spend rose **22.5% YoY** to ₹386 cr and is now a third of total expenditure — compounding at roughly twice underlying profit growth. On the call, the CFO gave the first explicit guidance: hold technology at **~8.7% of operating revenue, growing in the mid-teens**. Employee cost (+18% YoY) was flagged as a wage-code base effect that self-corrects from Q3. Capex run-rate ~₹450 cr a year, almost all technology.

That caps the bleed but does not reverse it: mid-teens cost growth on ~10% revenue growth still dilutes margin slowly. One natural hedge: SEBI regulatory fees fell 12% because they track notional turnover — model it as a variable cost.

## 5. What management said, and what they refused to say

- **Options share is really ~70%, not 68%** — the headline is depressed by NSE expiry days falling on holidays. Guided band **70–72%**, with ~100% share in monthly index and single-stock options; the loss is confined to weekly index options and "has stabilised over the last two or three quarters." *First time a share floor has been framed. Unverified.*
- **No expiry-frequency change coming** — but this rests entirely on the SEBI chair's public statements, not on a rule.
- **Refused:** what share of options revenue comes from expiry day — "an analysis we'll have to conduct." This is the single most important sensitivity in the model, and it is undisclosed.
- **No near-term data pricing lever.** Market Lens (~10,000 datasets) launches free; any future fee is framed as cost recovery, not profit.
- **AI** (GenAI in compliance, support, operations, software engineering, all in-house): "initial success," no numbers. Model it as zero.

## 6. Balance sheet and valuation

Zero debt. Corporate treasury (NSE's *own* money, excluding ₹17,914 cr client float, ₹13,665 cr Core SGF and ₹2,973 cr IPFT) is ₹34,699 cr. Book value per share fell to ₹117.84 from ₹129.75 at Mar-26 — the ₹35 dividend (84% payout) exceeded H1 EPS.

| At CMP ₹1,804 | NSE | BSE |
|---|---|---|
| P/E (TTM EPS ₹44.35) | ~41x | ~53–57x |
| P/B | 15.3x | ~21x |
| Normalised EBITDA margin | 76% | materially lower |
| Options premium share | 68% | ~32% |
| Underlying profit growth | ~9–10% | much faster, small base |

**Scenarios on FY27E:** Bear ₹1,376 (EPS ₹43 @ 32x, share slips below 68%, tech cost keeps compounding) · Base ₹1,702 (EPS ₹46 @ 37x) · Bull ₹2,037 (EPS ₹48.5 @ 42x, volumes recover to Q1 levels).

**Verdict — HOLD.** At ~40x normalised earnings for ~10% underlying growth you are buying an annuity, not growth: ~100% of equity futures, 92% of cash, 68% of options premium, 76% margins, no debt, ₹34,699 cr of own cash, a near-absolute regulatory moat and a ~2% yield. All of that is a fine thing to own — at a price. There is no margin of safety here. Accumulate below ~₹1,650. Telling detail: the stock is up just ~1% on its IPO cap price six weeks after listing, *before* a result headlining +23% PAT. The market had already priced out the base effect.

**Would make me pay up:** a third and fourth quarter of options share at or above 68%; data connectivity turning positive; non-transaction revenue crossing ~15% of operating revenue.
**Would make me sell:** options premium share breaking below 65% (October monthly data implies ~62–64% — BSE's premium grew 53–61% MoM vs NSE's 34%; flag to verify, not yet a fact); any adverse Supreme Court movement on the colocation **disgorgement** claim, which is unquantified and carries no provision; further F&O tightening.
